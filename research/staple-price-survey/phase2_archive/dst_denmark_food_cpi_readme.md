# Statistics Denmark (DST) CPI — food subgroups (PRIS01)

**Retrieved:** 2026-10-24 Asia/Tokyo · Aoi Engel companion

## What
Denmark **national** CPI by COICOP commodity group from StatBank table **PRIS01**, monthly **Index** (ENHED=100).
Food (01*), non-alcoholic beverages (012*), restaurants (11 / 111 / 1111) plus selected staple 5-/6-digit groups.

Distinct from Eurostat HICP food (`eurostat_hicp_food_panel.csv`, DNK). Retail **levels** for DNK remain open/miss.
