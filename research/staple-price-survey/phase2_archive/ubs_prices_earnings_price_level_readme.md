# UBS Prices and Earnings — city price levels (Hammar extract)

- **Panel:** `ubs_prices_earnings_price_level_panel.csv`
- **What:** City-year basket price levels (excl./incl. rent), index NYC 2015 = 100.
- **Primary source:** UBS *Prices and Earnings* surveys (1970–2018 editions), as compiled in Hammar & Waldenström (2020) *Global Earnings Inequality Database* v2.0, Tab4.
- **URL:** https://sites.google.com/site/ollehammar85/data
- **Raw:** `raw/product_indices_2026-10-25/olle_full.xlsx`
- **Coverage:** 89 cities, ~68 ISO3 countries, 1970–2018 (survey years uneven).
- **License / terms:** Research database distributed by the authors for academic use; cite Hammar & Waldenström and UBS as primary. Not UBS’s own open data portal (that microsite was discontinued).
- **Caveats:** Basket price *level*, not a single branded SKU. Not continuous annual for every city. No FX fills applied here. A Google Drive folder titled “UBS All Years” found in search was **not** UBS P&E (US residential property) — discarded.
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-25 (Asia/Tokyo box).
