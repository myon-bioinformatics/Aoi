# adjacent_extra — National food / bread-cereals CPI pack

Retrieved: 2026-10-24 Asia/Tokyo

## Scope

National (or NSO-redistributed) CPI for **food** and **bread & cereals** for Big Mac high-income gap countries and LatAm FPMA-thin countries in `countries_iso3.txt`.

**Japan CPI skipped** (Aoi lane). **FAO CPI bulk skipped** (other executor).

### Deferred to Aoi grokボット

- FAO Cost of a Healthy Diet / Food Prices for Nutrition (CoAHD)
- FAOSTAT agricultural producer prices (rice / wheat / maize)

## Hard rules

No FX conversion; no unit homogenization; no gap-filling. Vertical CSV columns:
`country,date,year,variable,value,unit,source,note,estimated_value`.

## Notes

- OECD/Eurostat series are redistributed national / HICP indices; base period recorded in `unit` / note.
- Argentina series are **GBA regional**, not a national aggregate (national file URLs returned HTML stubs).
- Multiple sources for the same country×variable×date may coexist (different `source`).
- `estimated_value` is always `false` in this pack.
