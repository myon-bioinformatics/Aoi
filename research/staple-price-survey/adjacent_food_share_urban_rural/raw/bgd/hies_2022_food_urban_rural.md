# Bangladesh HIES 2022 — food share urban/rural

Source: BBS HIES 2022 Key Findings (12 Apr 2023)
https://bbssbd.com/public/images/notices/645bde78c439d.pdf

Published household monthly means (Tk):

| geography | food consumption | total consumption | food share |
|-----------|-----------------:|------------------:|-----------:|
| Urban | 15,875 | 39,971 | 39.7% |
| Rural | 13,125 | 26,207 | 50.1% |
| National | 14,003 | 30,603 | 45.8% (published) |

Share = food ÷ consumption expenditure ×100 from BBS published means (food+non-food = consumption).
