# iPhone launch MSRP — 5 countries, generations X–16

- **Panel:** `iphone_launch_msrp_5country_panel.csv`
- **What:** Compiled **launch** prices for iPhone X through iPhone 16 families in Pakistan, India, China, USA, UAE (Dubai).
- **Source:** Public gist https://gist.github.com/pdotsani/e391e6f7c8f4cbfcaa458030d85dbb6a (`iphones_dataset.csv`).
- **USD:** Only USA rows (currency USD). Other countries keep local+currency; no FX fill.
- **observation_date:** `{launch_year}-09` approximate (source gives year only).
- **Caveats:** Compiled table, not independently verified against Apple archives; storage/config as labeled in gist.
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-25 (Asia/Tokyo box).
