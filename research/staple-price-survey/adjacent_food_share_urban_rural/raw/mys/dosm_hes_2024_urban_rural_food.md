# DOSM Household Expenditure Survey Report 2024 — urban/rural Food & Beverages share

PDF: https://storage.dosm.gov.my/hies/household_expenditure_2024.pdf

Narrative (EN § urban/rural composition, 2024):
- Urban Food & Beverages: **14.4%** of household consumption expenditure
- Rural Food & Beverages: **23.0%**

Corroborating table cells (Malay detailed tables):
- `01 Makanan dan Minuman` Bandar 14.4 / Luar Bandar 23.0 (e.g. lines with 879 14.4 / 864 23.0)

Note: Restaurants & Accommodation Services are a **separate** COICOP-like group (urban 17.4% / rural 14.9%) — not added into food_urban/rural here.
