# Orshansky / official poverty-line food-basket multipliers (verified only)

**Retrieved:** 2026-10-24 Asia/Tokyo · NORMATIVE historical notes — **do not mix with Engel E** without labeling.

Only multipliers with a clear official or primary historical citation are listed. No invented Ns. International poverty lines that do **not** publish an explicit food-basket multiplier are omitted.

## United States (official poverty thresholds — Orshansky lineage)

| N | Applies to | Basis (verified) | Citation |
|---:|---|---|---|
| **3** | Families of **3 or more** persons | 1955 USDA Household Food Consumption Survey: food ≈ 1/3 of after-tax money income for families of 3+ → threshold ≈ 3 × economy food plan cost | ASPE, *History of Poverty Thresholds*: https://aspe.hhs.gov/topics/poverty-economic-mobility/poverty-guidelines/further-resources-poverty-measurement-poverty-lines-their-history/history-poverty-thresholds ; Census Bureau, *How the Census Bureau Measures Poverty*: https://www.census.gov/topics/income-poverty/poverty/guidance/poverty-measures.html ; Fisher (SSA Bulletin) PDF: https://www.ssa.gov/policy/docs/ssb/v55n4/v55n4p3.pdf |
| **3.7** | **Two-person** families | Same survey: food ≈ 27% of income for two-person families → 1/0.27 ≈ **3.7** | Same ASPE / Census / SSA sources as above |
| *(derived, not a separate food×N)* | **One-person** units | Thresholds derived from two-person thresholds (not an independent food×multiplier in the original design) | ASPE history page; Census poverty measurement guidance |

**Notes**
- Economy food plan (USDA) was the food basket; multipliers convert food cost → poverty threshold.
- Census updates dollar thresholds with CPI-U; the **multipliers themselves** are historical design constants, not re-estimated annually.
- Label as **NORMATIVE / Orshansky** — distinct from observed Engel food shares (E).
