## Table II.3 — Structure of money expenditure of households in towns and rural areas

Food and non-alcoholic beverages (Продукти харчування та безалкогольні напої) % of average monthly household **money** expenditure.

| Year | Urban total (всього міських) | Rural (сільська місцевість) |

Urban = column "всього" under towns (not big/small city split). Rural money-food share is below urban because personal subsidiary farming (Витрати на особисте підсобне господарство) is ~4.6–5.2% of rural money expenditure and food in kind sits outside the money food share.
