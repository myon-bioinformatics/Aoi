# PHL FIES urban/rural food expenditure shares

PSA open HTML/press releases probed this turn do not publish a ready-made national food-share × urban/rural table.
Year-by-year food expenditure shares below are **authors' means from PSA FIES microdata**, published in:
Bairagi et al. (2022) PLOS ONE, Table 2 (PMC8970376) — "Food expenditure share" rows by survey year × rural/urban.

| year | urban | rural |
|-----:|------:|------:|
| 2006 | 0.46 | 0.54 |
| 2009 | 0.47 | 0.55 |
| 2012 | 0.47 | 0.56 |
| 2015 | 0.46 | 0.53 |
| 2018 | 0.47 | 0.53 |

Values stored as percent (×100). Definition: food expenditure ÷ total household expenditure (authors; includes food away from home in the food share row — see paper Table 2 vs "Food consumed outside household" separate row).
